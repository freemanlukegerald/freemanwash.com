FAQ

Good Questions.

What file formats do you accept?

PNG, PDF, SVG, AI, EPS — most common design files work. For best results, send vector files or high-res PNGs (at least 300 DPI).

Is there a minimum order?

Nope. Order as few as 1. Pricing scales down with quantity, but there's no minimum holding you back.

How long does it take?

Production takes 48 hours after proof approval. Shipping time depends on your location — typically 2–5 business days.

Do I get a proof before printing?

Yes — always. We send a digital proof for your approval before we run a single print. You control the final look.

Are the stickers waterproof?

Yes. All stickers are printed on waterproof, UV-resistant vinyl. They're built for the outdoors and everyday use.

Can you help with my design?

Send us what you have and we'll tell you honestly if it'll print well. We can make minor adjustments — or connect you with design help for bigger projects.
