Did You Know?

Your Trash Cans Are a Health Hazard

Most people never clean their bins. Here's why that's a problem.

🦠

Millions of Bacteria

Uncleaned bins harbor E. coli, salmonella, listeria, and other dangerous bacteria that can spread to your hands, home, and family.

🪲

Pests & Maggots

Food residue and standing liquid attract flies, roaches, and rodents. A clean bin eliminates the source that draws them in.

💨

Neighborhood Odors

Bin odor lingers even after trash day. Regular cleaning eliminates the buildup so your cans don't stink up your driveway or garage.

🏡

Curb Appeal

Clean bins look better on the curb and in the garage. It's a small detail that makes a real difference in how your home presents.
